Ember Knight Privacy Policy

Effective 23 October 2026

Ember Knight is a single-player game made by Bane (the "developer"). This policy explains what the game does with your information. The short version: the game does not collect, send or share any personal data.

What the game collects

Nothing. Ember Knight has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no in-app purchases. It does not use the internet at all, and the Android app does not ask for internet access.

What the game stores on your device

To remember where you are, the game saves two small files in its own private storage on your device:

  • Game progress: which levels you have unlocked, your best score and stars on each level, the skills you have learned and the medals you have earned.
  • Settings: sound, music, vibration, effects, easy mode, language and where the touch buttons sit.

These files are never sent to the developer or anyone else. If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include them in your own encrypted device backup, so your progress comes back when you reinstall the game or move to a new phone. That backup is handled by Android and Google under your account; the developer cannot see it. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, deletes the files on the phone.

Permissions

On Android the game asks for one permission, vibration, used only for short buzzes during play (for example when you land a hit, get hurt or reach a campfire). You can turn it off in the game's settings.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Third parties

The game contains no third-party code that collects data. Google Play itself may collect information when you download the game; that is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Changes

If a future version of the game starts collecting any data, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
