Raspored časova Privacy Policy

Effective 27 October 2026

Raspored časova (Android package com.bane23.classschedule) is a class schedule and school calendar app for primary-school children and their parents, made by Bane (the "developer"). This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: the app does not collect, send or share any personal data.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no in-app purchases. It does not use the internet at all, and it does not ask for internet access.

What the app stores on your device

Everything you enter is saved only in the app's own private storage on your device:

  • children's names, school, class timetables (morning and afternoon shift), subjects and bell times;
  • school calendar events, holidays and one-day schedule changes;
  • extracurricular activities (such as sports, choir or school trips) with their days, times, places and notes;
  • reminder and app settings, such as the "classes are over" alarm, the evening packing reminder, activity reminders, sounds and theme.

This data is never sent to the developer or anyone else. If you add the home-screen widget, it shows the day's classes on your home screen.

Permissions

  • Notifications: used only to show the "classes are over", "pack the bag" and activity reminders you turn on.
  • Alarms & reminders (exact alarms): used only so those reminders arrive at the exact minute.
  • Run at startup: used only to set the reminders again after the phone restarts or the time changes.
  • Camera: used only while you scan a timetable QR code shown on another phone. Camera images are not saved or sent anywhere.

Sharing a timetable by QR code

When you choose to share a child's timetable, the app shows the child's name, school, timetable, bell times, shift settings, calendar events, subject list and extracurricular activities as a QR code on your screen. Nothing leaves your phone unless someone scans that code with their own phone. The developer never receives this data.

Backups

If Android's device backup is turned on for your phone, Android may include the app's data in your personal backup (for example, your Google account backup). That backup is handled by Android and Google, not by the app, and the developer has no access to it.

Deleting your data

You can delete a child or any entry inside the app. To delete all data, uninstall the app or clear its storage in your phone's settings.

Children

The app is made for primary-school children (about 6 to 15 years old) and their parents, and children may use it on their own. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children: there are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no internet access. Children's names, timetables and activities entered in the app stay on the device where they were entered, unless a parent or the child shows a QR code that another phone scans. The app follows Google Play's Families policy.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party code that collects data. Google Play itself may collect information when you download the app; that is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Changes

If a future version of the app starts collecting any data, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
